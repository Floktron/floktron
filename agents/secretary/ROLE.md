# AI secretary

**For:** the owner or manager who loses time in the mailbox and in paperwork.

## Every day
| When | What the secretary does | Part |
|---|---|---|
| every 30 min | Reads the mailbox (read-only), puts every message into one sheet, finds who waits for your answer, nudges you in Telegram or by e-mail | [MAIL_WATCH](../../docs/MAIL_WATCH.md) |
| morning | Your calendar in the morning message; due dates as calendar files | [CALENDAR](../../docs/CALENDAR.md) |
| day 3 / 7 / 14 after an offer | A polite reminder to the buyer — sent only after your ✅ | [FOLLOWUPS](../../docs/FOLLOWUPS.md) |
| on request | Invoice, advance invoice, act or offer from the company library, in your brand | [LIBRARY](../../docs/LIBRARY.md), [DOCUMENTS](../../docs/DOCUMENTS.md) |
| when an invoice arrives | Checks it against the bank statement, tells finance what to pay or check | [INVOICES](../../docs/INVOICES.md), [GATEWAYS](../../docs/GATEWAYS.md) |
| when a customer writes to the bot | A ready lead in the table and a message to you | [TELEGRAM](../../docs/TELEGRAM.md) |

## Never by itself
Sends nothing to a customer without your OK · pays nothing · deletes, moves or forwards no mail · opens no link from a letter.

## What you get
Nothing waits unnoticed, documents look the same every time, and the AI reads summaries instead of the whole mailbox.
